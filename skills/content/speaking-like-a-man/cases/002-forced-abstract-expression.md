# Case 002: Forced Abstract Expression

## Source and privacy

The user selected a sentence from a generated personal review and identified a
forced expression. Relevant nearby context and generation provenance were
checked locally. The available provenance identifies a historical style snapshot
and application prose guidance; it does not establish that the complete MAN skill
or today's shared rules were loaded. Loading a historical style resource does
not establish a source defect or a violation of current character rules.

This is a wording-calibration case, not a reproduced failure of the current MAN
or character skills. Only the necessary short phrase is quoted. Names, source
paths, record identifiers, dates, exact preparation time and unrelated personal
details are omitted. The context below is a privacy-preserving paraphrase, not a
source quotation. All additional examples are newly constructed.

## Observed wording and feedback

> 也没有让失落轻一点。

The checked context contrasts knowing that preparation was limited with still
feeling disappointed by a result. It does not require a new psychological cause
or additional personal detail to explain the wording problem.

The user's objection is to the pairing “失落轻一点”. Familiar words can form a
strained combination; the issue is neither the sentence form “让……一点” nor
monosyllabic adjectives in general. The user prefers an ordinary collocation
such as “减轻失落” or “缓解失落”, with a degree modifier only when useful.

A sanitized reconstruction and two possible revisions are:

- Reconstructed context: “知道准备时间有限，也没有让失落轻一点。”
- Familiar verb: “知道准备时间有限，也没有减轻你的失落。”
- Direct phrasing: “虽然知道准备时间有限，你还是很失落。”

Choose by meaning, voice and surrounding syntax, not by matching either revision.
Preserve the contrast between knowing the limitation and feeling disappointed;
do not invent relief, acceptance or an emotional explanation.

## Related expressions: reviewed scope, new examples

The user also approved examining images that do no work beyond making the prose
sound tender or profound. Examples such as “让委屈薄一点” or “把焦虑放软一点”
can be awkward when ordinary verbs express the intended change more naturally.
An image such as “给遗憾找个位置” needs context: what attitude, action or relation
does it express, and would that meaning disappear if the image were removed?
If it merely decorates a generic reassurance, revise it. If it develops a
specific image or a meaningful shift, keep it. These constructed examples do
not establish additional observed failures or warrant automatic replacement.

## Preserve natural and purposeful expression

- “让心情好一点” is ordinary speech; its sentence form is not a defect.
- “心里一沉” is a natural idiom; its physical image is not grounds for removal.
- Weight, depth, softness or placing can support a sustained image or a deliberate
  character voice. Judge their actual function, rather than treating them as
  inherently artificial or demanding literal wording throughout.

## Review criteria

- Does the revision use a natural collocation while preserving the emotional
  relation, uncertainty and supplied facts?
- Does an image add meaning, development or voice, rather than just a gentle or
  profound surface? Are conventional and contextually effective expressions kept?
- Are verified context, sanitized paraphrase, constructed examples and user
  judgment distinguished without retaining private locators?
- Does the review separate an expression concern from a loaded-rule violation
  and an unproven source-defect hypothesis?

Judge the result in context. This case supplies neither a word blacklist nor
evidence of universal improvement or live platform-model validation.
