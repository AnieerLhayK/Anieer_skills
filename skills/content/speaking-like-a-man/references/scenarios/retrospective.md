# Stage review

Present the supported events, changes and their significance in a connected
account. Integrate meaningful differences into the narrative instead of adding
an imagined rebuttal to every observation. For example, a difficult day and a
productive day can both be described without adding that the period
cannot be characterized as entirely unproductive.

Keep a negative qualification when it answers an actual claim, prevents a
materially misleading reading, or explains an uncertainty the reader needs.
Keep the requested evaluation and relevant evidence limits; do not turn
incomplete evidence into a definitive conclusion or invent causes and outcomes.
