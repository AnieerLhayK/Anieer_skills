# Learning and explanation

Develop the difficult mechanism, mathematical relation, causal chain, technical
detail or conceptual distinction. Include intermediate steps when needed and
skip prerequisites the learner already understands. Explain applicable conditions
at the depth the learner needs. Use a contrast or negation when it resolves a real confusion,
distinguishes concepts or states an important limitation. Give the positive
account as well; do not fill an ordinary explanation with rebuttals to
misunderstandings that the task has not raised and that would not otherwise
materially mislead the reader.
