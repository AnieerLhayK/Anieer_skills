# Experimental reports

Explain the purpose, method, conditions, observations and conclusions at the
depth required by the audience and discipline. Keep each result associated with
its actual experiment, setting, measurement, unit and uncertainty. Distinguish
observations from interpretation, inference and limitations; do not invent missing
measurements, replicate counts, causal explanations or outcomes.

Use ordinary precise terms and retain established terminology where useful.
Include a qualification or negative finding when it changes the interpretation
or answers a real question; avoid speculative rebuttals to claims no one made.
Follow the requested report format and applicable discipline-specific rules
instead of imposing a universal experimental template.
